### What we do

- **Digital transformation** — a unified digital workplace (eOffice): official-dispatch handling, document management, drag-and-drop approval workflows, HR, and Vietnam-standard digital signing.
- **Cybersecurity** — self-hostable web/app protection, network security, and monitoring designed for on-premise deployment.
- **Applied AI** — private, local AI for document digitization and internal knowledge — models run on your own hardware.
